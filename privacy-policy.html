Privacy Policy

Effective 12 August 2026

Solar Engineer is a small, independent operation — this policy is written to actually describe what happens to your data, not to hedge every sentence for a company far bigger than this one.

01Who controls your data

Luke Stevenson, trading as Solar Engineer, a UK-based sole trader, is the data controller for the information described below. Contact: lukey20041@outlook.com.

02What's collected, and why

DataSourceWhy
Name, email, payment details Gumroad, at purchase Deliver the guide, handle receipts/refunds. Payment details are processed by Gumroad directly — never received or stored by Solar Engineer.
Email, quote details (if provided) You, via email or a booking form Provide the paid quote-review or consultation service you requested.
TikTok account authorization token TikTok, via OAuth when the account is connected Publish Solar Engineer's own video content to its own TikTok account via the Content Posting API. Not used to read followers, messages, or other users' content.

03TikTok data specifically

The TikTok integration requests only the permissions needed to publish video content to the connected Solar Engineer account. It does not collect, read, or store data about your followers, comments, or any other TikTok user or content. The access token is stored securely and only used to make posting requests you've initiated; you can revoke it at any time from your TikTok account's connected-apps settings, which immediately cuts off access.

04Third parties

Payment and digital delivery run through Gumroad, and video publishing runs through TikTok's own API — both are independent data controllers for the data they process on their platforms; their own privacy policies apply to that handling.

Solar Engineer does not sell your data, to advertisers, installers, or anyone else — including under any future installer-referral arrangement, which would be disclosed separately and only ever cover a referral fee, not a data sale.

05How long data is kept

Purchase and correspondence records are kept as long as needed for UK tax/accounting obligations (currently up to 6 years), then deleted. TikTok authorization tokens are deleted immediately if you disconnect the integration, or if it's been inactive for an extended period.

06Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data held about you, by emailing lukey20041@outlook.com. Requests are handled directly — there's no automated system standing between you and a real answer.
